# Articles (a, an, the)

Source: https://developers.google.com/style/articles

Include definite and indefinite articles (*a*, *an*, *the*) for ease of comprehension and translation. Don't drop articles for brevity, including in headings and titles.

- Recommended: "Create a VM instance"
- Not recommended: "Create VM instance"

See also: Write for a global audience (standard English word order), Headings and titles, Articles before product names, and Indefinite articles before abbreviations (choosing *a*/*an* when an abbreviation's pronunciation varies).
