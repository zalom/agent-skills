# Philosophy of this guide

Source: https://developers.google.com/style/philosophy

## Intended purpose

This style guide codifies and records style decisions and describes the house style. The guide does not claim to be objectively correct.

This guide is *not* intended to do the following:

- Provide an industry documentation standard.
- Compete with other well-known style guides.
- Replace another style guide that you already follow.
- Provide a complete set of basic writing guidelines.
- Provide legal advice. For legal advice, consult a lawyer.

**Note**: Two disclaimers:

- The guidance in this style guide does not limit the changes that Google can make to its documentation.
- If you don't read a given guideline, then you are still responsible for behaving ethically and lawfully with regard to documentation.

## Explanation of reasons for guidelines

Generally, reasoning behind most guidelines is not explained. There are several reasons for that:

- Many decisions are driven by accessibility, localization, globalization, and ease of understanding. Providing those reasons everywhere they apply would be repetitive.
- Often, a given guideline is one good option among several; in those cases, one option was chosen for consistency.
- Too much explanation can clutter up a page. Readers most often want a brief answer to a specific question, rather than a detailed explanation.

That said, it is sometimes useful to know why a given choice was made, so occasional explanations are included in the What's new page.
