# Contractions

Source: https://developers.google.com/style/contractions

Documentation uses an informal tone — common two-word contractions (*you're*, *don't*, *there's*) are recommended.

## Negation contractions
Recommended, especially *isn't*, *don't*, *can't* — readers scanning text can easily miss a standalone "not," but "don't" is harder to misread as "do." To emphasize a negative, use formatting like "is <em>not</em>" (renders "is *not*") — but usually unnecessary.

## Contractions to avoid
- No nonstandard contractions (*guides're*, or *browser's* meaning "browser is").
- No three-word contractions (*mightn't've*).
