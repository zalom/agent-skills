# Slashes
Source: https://developers.google.com/style/slashes

Avoid using slashes, except in code, file paths, and URLs.

## Slashes with dates
Don't use slash-based date formats. See Dates and times for proper formats.

## Slashes with alternatives
Don't use slashes to separate alternatives — use "or"/"and" instead.
- Recommended: "...has been developed and is hosted by a commercial entity." (both true)
- Recommended: "...has been developed or is hosted by a commercial entity." (either true)
- Not recommended: "...has been developed/hosted by a commercial entity."
- Recommended: "Call this method five or six times." — Not recommended: "Call this method 5/6 times."

### And/or
*And* often implies *or*, so both words are rarely needed. Avoid "and/or" except when space is limited (e.g., tables).
- Recommended: "You can view and edit your own data." — Not recommended: "You can view and/or edit your own data."
- Recommended: "You can export raw events, processed events, or both." — Not recommended: "You can export raw and/or processed events."

## Slashes with file paths and URLs
Use forward slashes in file paths and URLs (backslashes for Windows paths). Break long URLs immediately after a slash — never insert an extraneous hyphen to break a URL.
- Recommended: "https://developers.google.com/cardboard/" broken as "https://developers.google.com/\ncardboard/"

## Slashes with fractions
Don't use slashes for fractions — ambiguous (e.g., "3/4" could mean three-quarters or "4" as an alternative to "3").
- Recommended: ¾ / 0.75 / 75% — Not recommended: 3/4

## Slashes with abbreviations
Don't use slash-based abbreviations — spell the words out.
- Recommended: "care of," "with" — Not recommended: "c/o," "w/"
