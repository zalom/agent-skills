# Third-Party Content

Source: https://developers.google.com/style/other-sources

Don't copy content from another source — it might violate copyright. Instead, paraphrase and link to the original. This applies to text, images, code, logos, and speech.

| Recommended | Not recommended |
|---|---|
| "A recovery point objective (RPO), which is the maximum acceptable length of time during which data might be lost from your app due to a major incident." | "Recovery Point Objective (RPO): 'RPO is the maximum targeted period in which data (transactions) might be lost from an IT service due to a major incident' (wikipedia.org/...)" |

## Sources to avoid copying from (unless you're certain your company owns the assets)
- **Third-party sources** — documentation, websites, books, blogs, videos, images, podcasts, and more.
- **Reference sources** — dictionaries, encyclopedias, Wikipedia.
- **Open source product documentation** — OSS licenses vary widely (from no-reuse-without-attribution to fully permissive); never assume free reuse. When in doubt, don't use it.
- **GitHub content** — different GitHub users adopt different licenses; never assume free reuse. When in doubt, don't use it.
